AI sticks when it clears a bottleneck.

Most people try AI on whatever comes to mind, get a so-so answer and go back to the old way. It stays when it is pointed at the thing costing you time or money every single week.

Why we start with the bottleneck. Harvard's Clayton Christensen showed that people don't buy products. They hire them to make progress in a particular situation, and fire them when they stop helping.

A business owner doesn't want "AI". They want the enquiry answered, the invoice chased and Friday afternoon back. So we name the job first, then hire AI for it.

Questions owners ask

AI for small business: plain answers.

What is AI Academy for Beginners?

AI Academy for Beginners is a UK-based AI training platform for small business owners and non-technical professionals. It teaches you to use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and simple automation to clear specific business bottlenecks, with no coding. Its 50,000+ member community is led by Sam Onigbanjo, an Enterprise Nation Accredited AI Adviser.

What is The Bottleneck Clinic?

The Bottleneck Clinic is the academy's £19 a month membership. It includes 27 self-paced lessons, a live session with Sam at the end of every month, a Template Vault of prompts and scripts, and a weekly scorecard that tracks offers sent, leads collected, calls booked and cash collected. You can cancel anytime from your account.

What are the most common bottlenecks for small businesses?

The most common are winning customers and growing sales, admin taking up the week, rising costs and unpredictable cash flow, red tape, the cost of skilled staff, and the owner becoming the bottleneck. UK owners report spending around 11 hours a week on admin and finance tasks, nearly twice the time they spend on selling (American Express and Small Business Saturday UK, 2026).

Can AI help a small business with admin?

Yes. Admin is one of the jobs AI does best. It can draft emails and replies, summarise meetings, write reports and tidy data. Most owners start with the single task they repeat most each week, hand it to AI, and check the result before it goes out.

Can AI answer my business phone calls?

Yes. A voice AI agent can answer calls in a natural voice, handle common questions, qualify callers and book appointments, then pass anything unusual to a person. Building one is taught in Module 07 of the full programme.

Do I need coding or technical skills to learn AI?

No. Everything is taught in plain English. If you can copy and paste, you can follow every step.

How much time does it take?

Plan for about two hours a week. The aim is that the bottleneck you clear gives you back more time than that.

Growth, at £550 a month, adds bi-weekly coaching with Sam's team and a certificate of completion. Elite, at £950 a month by application, adds a monthly 1-to-1 with Sam. All plans are monthly.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. Every plan has a 30-day money-back guarantee. Work through the first two modules and put them into practice. If it hasn't delivered value, email hello@aiacademyforbeginners.com within 30 days for a full refund.

Do you offer AI training for teams?

Yes. For groups of five or more, email partnerships@aiacademyforbeginners.com about team packages. Most teams start with the governance module, which gives you an AI usage policy to roll out before anyone uses client data.

Which AI tools will I learn?

ChatGPT, Claude and Google Gemini, plus voice and video AI and no-code automation. We teach which tool fits which job, rather than one version of one tool, because features and prices change often.

Is it safe to use AI with my business data?

It can be, if you know the rules. The governance module covers data protection, GDPR and copyright in plain English, with a usage policy template you can adapt for your team.

What does the Jobs to Be Done approach mean for learning AI?

Jobs to Be Done is Clayton Christensen's idea that people hire a product to make progress in a particular situation. Applied to AI, it means you start with the job that's stuck in your business, such as following up leads, and choose the tool for that job, instead of learning tools and hoping to find a use for them.
